Contact


The best way to contact board staff is to create a thread in the Minecraft Java Speedrunning discord (more commonly referred to as Javacord). There used to be an email but it has unfortunately been lost to time. DMing an active moderator or verifier on speedrun.com/mc may also work, but it is just easier to miss or forget about.


How to create a thread


in the #help channel, a thread can be opened by clicking the icon in the top right and clicking “Create.” The thread will be created once you send a message. Please give your thread a descriptive name.
